05 — A FAMILY JOURNEY

Across borders.
Still together.

Joseph Obadiah Bibi was remembered as a master engraver and silversmith. His work took him as far as Bombay. In 1910, he and his son Reuben left Beirut for Tunis; the family story then passes through Lyon, Ellis Island, San Francisco, and finally New York.

The record places Joseph on Ellis Island in 1915 and in New York by 1918. A route measured in years and cities became a new chapter for the Bibi family in Brooklyn.
